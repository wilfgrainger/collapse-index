About

Pressure, not prophecy.

The UK Stability Monitor is an open, reproducible measure of observable pressure on the UK's economic, household, institutional, public-service and environmental systems, built only from published evidence whose exact bytes are hashed and kept.

What it means, and what it does not

What the monitor claims and does not claim
It isIt is not
A fixed-weight summary of measured strainA forecast that the country will collapse
Recalculated daily from the latest releasesA daily statistic: most inputs are monthly, quarterly or annual
Explicit about missing evidence and its weightA complete picture while indicators are missing
Traceable to official source series and payload hashesAn official statistic or a government product
Separate about confidence and pressureA political approval rating or sentiment score

How to read the dashboard

  • Measured pressure is the fixed-weight sum of collected indicators on a 0–100 scale. With indicators missing it is a lower bound, not a score.
  • The range is where a complete score could fall if every missing indicator were at zero or at maximum pressure.
  • Confidence describes evidence health — quality, freshness and geographic coverage. It is not a statistical confidence interval.
  • The headline appears only when at least 90% of fixed weight is available and confidence is at least 70%. Until then it is withheld on purpose.
  • Each indicator page shows the source series, reference period, publication date, evidence hash, scoring curve and every stored version.

Questions

Why is there no headline number?
Because too little of the fixed weight is measured. Rescaling the measured indicators to look complete would turn a gap in the evidence into a claim about the country. The evidence page shows exactly how far each gate is from passing.
Why does the number move on a day when nothing was published?
It should not move without a new or revised observation, an approved event or a methodology change. Freshness can still age with time, which changes confidence, not pressure. Each day's materialisation is recorded on the history page.
Why is there no long historical chart?
History starts at the first live ingestion. A backcast needs source histories, published transformations and marked structural breaks; until that work is done no line is drawn.
Who decides the weights and curves?
They are fixed in a versioned, public methodology. Any change requires a new version, updated tests and an entry in the decision log. Independent statistical review is required before an editorial launch.
Can news events move the score?
Only through a separate, capped and decaying overlay, and only after named review with corroboration. That workflow is not built yet, so no event currently affects anything.
Can I reuse the data?
Yes. Code is MIT-licensed; source data is Crown copyright under the Open Government Licence v3.0 and must keep its attribution. Start with the export manifest.

Corrections and revision policy

Source revisions

When a publisher revises a value, the monitor stores a new observation version marked revised that points at the version it replaced. The original is never overwritten or deleted, and the snapshot for each day records exactly which version it used. Revisions are shown on each indicator page and in the observation export.

Errors in this monitor

If a collector, transformation or calculation is wrong, the fix is made in code with a regression test, the methodology or parser version is advanced where outputs change, and the change is recorded in the public decision log and progress record. Affected materialisations remain in the evidence store; corrected ones are added, never substituted silently.

Operational failures

If collection or the daily materialisation stops, the site says so: snapshots older than two days are labelled stale and the health endpoints report degraded. A failing evidence store is never masked with bundled data.

Reporting a problem

Report data or methodology errors as a GitHub issue with the indicator, period and what you expected. Report security vulnerabilities privately as described in SECURITY.md.
