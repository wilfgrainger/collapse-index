Guardrails

Built to be checkable, not dramatic

  • Fixed weights: missing data lowers coverage, never inflates what remains.
  • A blank source cell stays blank. It is never read as zero.
  • An observation cannot exist without the hash of the bytes it came from.
  • Rolling three-month estimates are stored with their true reference period.
  • England-only evidence carries a population factor and cannot pass as UK-wide.
  • Publication gates are public: 90% of fixed weight and 70% confidence.
